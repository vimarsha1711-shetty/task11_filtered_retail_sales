# Task 11 – Basic Data Sorting & Filtering

## Objective
To sort and filter a retail sales dataset and find useful information from the data.

## Dataset
The dataset contains retail sales information such as:
- Transaction ID
- Date
- Gender
- Age
- Age Group
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount

## Work Done
- Sorted the retail sales data.
- Applied filters to the dataset.
- Analysed product categories and sales.
- Compared sales by gender.
- Checked quantity sold.
- Identified the highest and lowest transaction amounts.

## Questions & Answers

### 1. Which product category has the highest total sales?
**Answer:** Electronics — 156,905

### 2. Which gender has the highest total sales?
**Answer:** Female — 232,840

### 3. Which product category has the highest quantity sold?
**Answer:** Clothing — 894 units

### 4. What is the highest transaction amount?
**Answer:** 2,000

### 5. What is the lowest transaction amount?
**Answer:** 25

## Conclusion
The retail sales data was sorted and filtered to identify important sales information. The analysis helped to understand sales by product category, gender, quantity, and transaction amount.
